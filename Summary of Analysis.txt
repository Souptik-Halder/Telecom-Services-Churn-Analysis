Feature Analysis:

Demographic Features:
Senior citizens have a different churn behavior than non-seniors.
Gender does not significantly impact churn.
Service Features:
Customers without additional services (like Online Security, Backup, and Tech Support) are more likely to churn.
Fiber optic internet users have higher churn rates than DSL users.
Contract Type & Churn:
Customers on monthly contracts have a much higher churn rate than those on yearly contracts.
Payment Methods:
Customers using electronic check payments tend to churn more compared to those using credit card or bank transfers.
Correlation Analysis:

A heatmap (sns.heatmap(df.corr())) was likely used to check feature relationships.
Monthly charges show a positive correlation with churn.
Key Insights:
Customers on monthly contracts, fiber optic internet, and electronic check payments have higher churn rates.
Customers with additional services (Online Security, Backup, Tech Support) tend to stay longer.
Demographics (age, gender) do not strongly impact churn.
High monthly charges contribute to customer churn.
