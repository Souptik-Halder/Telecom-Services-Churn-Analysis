## TELECOM SERVICES CHURN ANALYSIS
Churn Rate Distribution:
Churned Customers: ~26%
Retained Customers: ~74%
🔹 This indicates that about a quarter of customers leave the service.
Key Factors Influencing Churn:
Contract Type & Churn Rate:

Monthly Contract: ~43% churn
One-Year Contract: ~11% churn
Two-Year Contract: ~3% churn
✅ Longer contracts significantly reduce churn.

![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/caa67590-b80e-4009-895c-209a5f7c4c1a)
Internet Service & Churn:

Fiber Optic: ~42% churn
DSL: ~23% churn
No Internet: ~7% churn
✅ Fiber optic users are more likely to leave.
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5799567d-c806-44fd-8e93-b66ab9ef1504)
Additional Services & Churn Reduction:

Tech Support Subscribers: ~15% churn
No Tech Support: ~42% churn
Online Security Subscribers: ~17% churn
No Online Security: ~41% churn
✅ Providing additional services increases retention.
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb0d22aa-8f93-4100-8843-29720d9b2ff2)
Payment Method & Churn:

Electronic Check Users: ~45% churn
Credit Card/Bank Transfer Users: ~16% churn
✅ Customers paying via electronic check are at the highest risk.
![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5de022eb-36d1-4e37-a9c6-b8bfcea7bdee)
Monthly Charges & Churn:

Customers paying above $70/month have 40%+ churn
Customers paying below $30/month have 10% churn
✅ Higher monthly bills increase churn risk.
